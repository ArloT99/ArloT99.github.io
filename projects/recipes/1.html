Zucchini Bread

This is a recipe for Zucchini Bread, my mom makes it sometimes, I think it's pretty good

"This is the recipe that my childhood friends ask me for"

Tips

  • Squeeze as much moisture out of zuchinni as possible

This makes 2 loaves of bread

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Eggs 3
Flour 2 c.
Sugar 2 c.
Oil 3/4 c.
Salt 1 tsp
Grated Zuchinni 2 c.
Baking powder 1/2 tsp
Baking soda 2 tsp
Cinnamon 2 tsp

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350
  3. Combine all dry ingredients and whisk together
  4. In a seperate bowl beat the eggs and mix the oil and zuchinni
  5. Mix the wet and dry ingredients and put them into the two pans
  6. Bake for 50 minutes
