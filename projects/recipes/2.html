Tips

  • You can swap the corned beef with turkey

This makes 4-6 sandwiches

Ingredients

Ingredient Amount
Corned Beef 1/4 lb
Swiss Cheese 1/2 lb
Sliced Rye 1 loaf
Thousand Island Dressing 4 tb
Crispy Sauerkraut 1 c.
Mayo 1/4 c.

Instructions

  1. Put mayo on one side of bread and thousand island on other
  2. Put a slice of swiss on the thousand island side
  3. Put 3-4 slices of corned beef on top of the swiss
  4. Put 1/4 c. sauerkraut
  5. Put a slice of swiss on top of the sauerkraut
  6. Put thousand island on one side of the second slice and mayo on the other side, and put the slice on top of the sandwich
  7. Toast on a frying pan until golden brown
  8. Repeat 4-6 times
